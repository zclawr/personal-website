SimulCost: A Cost-Aware Benchmark and Toolkit for Automating Physics Simulations with LLMs

Yadi Cao, Sicheng Lai, Jiahe Huang, Yang Zhang, Zach Lawrence, Rohan Bhakta, Izzy F. Thomas, Mingyun Cao, Chung-Hao Tsai, Zihao Zhou, Yidong Zhao, Hao Liu, Alessandro Marinoni, Alexey Arefiev, Rose Yu

International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2026

arXiv Code

Benchmarks for LLM agents in science usually count tokens but ignore the cost of the tools the agent calls, which for physics simulations can mean hours of compute per attempt. SimulCost is a benchmark and toolkit that asks LLMs to choose cost-sensitive simulation parameters (mesh resolution, time step, and so on) that hit an accuracy target, and scores them on both success and the simulation compute they spend. It covers thousands of single-round and multi-round tuning tasks across twelve simulators from fluid dynamics, solid mechanics, and plasma physics. Frontier models make unreliable initial guesses and, even with trial-and-error feedback, end up slower than a conventional parameter scan, which points to cost-aware agent design as an open problem.

Deep Learning for Modeling and Dispatching Hybrid Wind Farm Power Generation

Zach Lawrence, Jessica Yao, Chris Qin

IEEE International Conference on Data Mining Workshops (ICDMW), 2025

arXiv

Hybrid wind farms pair turbines with energy storage, so an operator can decide when to bank power and when to sell it. This paper develops two deep learning frameworks for individual farms. COVE-NN is an LSTM-based dispatch policy trained without labeled dispatch decisions to directly maximize the value of the energy sold; in a 43-year case study at the Pyron site it cut the annual cost of valued energy by 32.3% relative to conventional baseload dispatch. The second is a probabilistic power generation model driven by atmospheric conditions, which reduced RMSE by 9.5% and improved power curve similarity by 18.9% on the Palouse wind farm, and can produce synthetic generation data for training more robust dispatch strategies.
